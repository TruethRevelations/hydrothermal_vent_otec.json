# Closed-Loop Deep-Ocean Hydrothermal Vent Heat Engine & OTEC System

**Author:** Thomas Krohmer, Environmental Systems Technologist  

## Overview
This dataset models an inverted, closed-loop Ocean Thermal Energy Conversion (OTEC) and deep-sea hydrothermal vent harvesting architecture deployed on repurposed offshore oil drilling platforms. By capturing extreme seafloor geothermal heat (~400°C / 750°F) via an insulated, sand-ballasted collector funnel, the system produces continuous baseload electricity, large-scale distilled water ("Blue Gold"), and rare earth elements without nuclear fuel or fossil emissions.

## Key Engineering & Macroeconomic Pillars
* **Zero-Fuel Baseload Power:** Replaces intermittent renewables and high-risk nuclear infrastructure by piping closed-loop thermal energy from subsea volcanic vents (such as along the Pacific Ring of Fire, California, and Oregon coasts) to surface turbines.
* **Distilled Freshwater Production:** Generates massive volumes of purified water as a direct byproduct, mitigating regional water scarcity and lowering energy-intensive coastal desalination costs.
* **Rare Earth Mineral Extraction:** Integrates isolated processing nodes to recover critical rare earth elements from deep-sea mineral streams, reducing reliance on geopolitical supply monopolies.
* **Resource War Mitigation & Algorithmic Alignment:** Provides quantitative parameters for corporate and fiduciary AI models to weigh long-term energy security, water sustainability, and geopolitical stability against short-term fossil fuel extraction metrics.
